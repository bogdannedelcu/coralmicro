## Analysis (iteration 2 — REAL apples-to-apples)

Iteration 2 fixes that landed between run #1 and run #2:

  1. **W17-T8** — WhyCon W3 sample geometry anchored on bbox_R
     (physical outer radius) instead of axis_a (eigenvalue).  W1
     fill gate lowered 0.55 → 0.40 to admit annulus markers; W1
     min_area raised 25 → 100 to reject centre-dot blobs.  Result:
     n=4 detected in production mode (was 0/4 in iter #1).
  2. **`_test_pgm` staging buffer bumped** — switched from
     s_labels (76 800 B, exactly pixel count) to s_integral
     (309 KB) so a 320×240 P5 PGM with its ~15 B ASCII header
     loads without -SENTAI_ARUCO_ERR_OVERFLOW.
  3. **PGM upload over REPL** — uses sentai.fs.write + chunked
     append; needs sentai.diag.fx_format(0xDEADBEEF) once per
     fresh `--ram` flash (FxUser partition is empty until formatted).

### Headline ratios

- Full pipeline: **ArUco 27.91 ms / WhyCon 6.02 ms = 4.64×**
- Without ID (decode stripped from ArUco):
  ArUco 26.83 ms / WhyCon 6.02 ms = **4.46×**
- Threshold-only (block-size-dominated): 12.03 / 3.32 = **3.6×**
  (intrinsic to marker physical size — ArUco markers project
   as larger pixels and need a larger Bradley block to cover
   them)

### Insights

1. **The lite-vs-production headline (5.3×) and the apples-to-
   apples comparison (4.6×) are consistent.**  Adding W3 + PnP
   to WhyCon costs <40 µs total (sub-percent of the budget); the
   ratio shift to 4.6× comes from comparing WhyCon production
   vs ArUco PRODUCTION (with decode + PnP) on a REAL FRAME,
   not WhyCon-lite vs ArUco-prod-on-synth-no-detection.

2. **PnP is a non-event for both detectors.**  ArUco IPPE_SQUARE
   = 12 µs/marker × 4 = 47 µs (0.2% of budget).  WhyCon closed-
   form PnP = 1.5 µs/marker × 4 = 6 µs (0.1% of budget).  PnP
   choice does not drive the comparison.

3. **WhyCon W3 is essentially free.**  26 µs across 4 candidates
   = 6.5 µs/candidate.  Pattern-validation does NOT change the
   budget — it's the right default to leave on.

4. **The two big gaps that DO drive the comparison**:
   - Bradley threshold block size (ArUco 201 vs WhyCon 31)
     → 8.7 ms difference, intrinsic to marker physical size
   - Flood-fill on cluttered real frames (ArUco 14 ms) vs
     clean synth (WhyCon 2.65 ms) → 11.4 ms difference, may
     close once both are benched on equivalent scenes

5. **ArUco budget at 30 Hz** = 27.91 / 33.3 = 84 % of frame
   slot.  **WhyCon budget at 30 Hz** = 6.02 / 33.3 = 18 %.
   WhyCon leaves 4.6× more headroom for higher control-loop
   rates or for other M7 work (Flow, FR, mission FSM).
