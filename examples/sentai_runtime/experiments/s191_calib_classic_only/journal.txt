## What we LEARNED across iter-1..6

1. **PnP yaw is unreliable** — flips between mirror solutions per
   frame.  Cannot use for yaw lock or for body→world rotation.
   Future: use cf2 gyro yaw (sentai.crazy.pose()[3]) OR add hysteresis.

2. **Axis identification is REQUIRED** — pitch/roll → X/Y mapping
   is NOT identity.  May be swapped, may have wrong sign.
   Calibration must DETERMINE this in-flight via test pulses.

3. **Gentle gains AT FIRST** — operator-stated: "miscari foarte mici"
   for axis ID, otherwise destabilize.  Recovery requires OPPOSITE
   pulse to return drone to start.

4. **Phase4 → Phase5 transition has residual velocity** — drone
   carries momentum into XY hold.  Need to damp velocity BEFORE
   testing axis sign.
