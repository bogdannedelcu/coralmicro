E2 — EdgeTPU model load latency.
Measures how long sentai.tpu.load() takes to load a .tflite model into EdgeTPU SRAM.

Columns:
  run_index      : repetition number (0-based)
  model_load_ms  : wall time of tpu.load() in milliseconds

Note: heap snapshots before/after are in the result dict but not in the CSV.

Parameters:
  model_path = /yolo26n.edgetpu_1.tflite
  repetitions = 3
