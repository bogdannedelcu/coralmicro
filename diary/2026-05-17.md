## Why this exists

The CrazySim cf2 model has a Gazebo plugin
(`gz-sim-odometry-publisher-system`) that publishes ground-truth
pose on `/cf_<id>/odom` at 200 Hz, and `crazysim_plugin.cpp:
OdomCallback` forwards each pose to cf2 firmware as a
`CrtpExtPose_s` external-pose update.  The cf2 EKF was therefore
running on PERFECT GT, not on real IMU + flow + vision.

Every flow/PnP baseline we've ever cited (s127 FlowBaseline 7.4 cm,
s130 1.2 cm, s142 1.8 cm closure, s147..s150 8-11 cm closures, s164
1m square 1.83 cm, s165 EKF-vs-GT 0.5 cm) was MEASURING NOISE
AROUND THE CHEAT, not real-stack capability.

Discovered while implementing OP-S10-W11-T5 (1m square drift test);
operator's anti-cheat scepticism led to the plugin grep.

## Empirical findings worth keeping

- **PnP-z lock works**: with the cheat off and the marker-pose table
  fixed, `z_PnP − z_EKF ≈ 0.74 cm` (down from 60–85 cm without
  the fix, but PnP is producing real Z and VPE pulls EKF into
  agreement).
- **Without VPE, IMU + flow + baro alone**: hover drift ≈ 9–11 cm
  / 15 s (3 trials).  This is what the post-cheat baseline looks
  like with vision absent.
- **With VPE noisy + aggressive PID**: drone oscillates violently
  (observed dist = 0.67 m in one trial).  Gentle PID is mandatory.
- **cf2 spawn is NOT reproducible**: trial ended with cf2 physically
  at (+0.57, −0.92, 0.015) — drone landed somewhere away from
  origin and the next trial's "respawn" was racy.  T6 priority.
- **Detection holds at z = 1 m**: 6 cm markers project to ~35 px,
  comfortably above OpenCV's ≈12–15 px detect floor.  Detection
  failures observed in late trials were because drone drifted out
  of the marker pad FOV, not because markers were too small.
