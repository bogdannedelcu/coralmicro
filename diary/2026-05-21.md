### Open problem 1: WhyCon detector misses markers (CRITICAL)

User-observed (visual): all 6 markers clearly visible in every captured
frame.  Detector reports: n_det = 0..6, varying per frame.  Specific
test on `t346121017_n2_f000285.pgm` (saved 6-marker frame from iter-19):

```
[whycon_filter] n_comp=96 accepted=2  rejects:
  border=14 min_area=72 max_area=1 ar=5 fill=1 axis=0 w3=1
  thresholds: min_a=100 max_a=4000 max_ar=1.50 min_fill=0.40 max_axis=2.00
```

scipy connected-components on the same frame (thresh < 50) finds the 6
outer rings cleanly: comps 6, 7, 13, 30, 34, 35 — each n_pix ≈ 750,
centers (88, 77), (210, 76), (149, 92), (149, 183), (89, 199), (211, 199).

Lowering `min_area` 100 → 40 added 1 more candidate (still ar-rejected);
raising `max_bbox_ar` 1.5 → 2.0 let 4 more candidates pass → but they
got rejected by W3 (3) + fill (3).  Net: still only 2 accepted.

**Hypothesis**: the Bradley rolling-integral threshold (block=31) used
by `whycon_detect_inplace_` produces a *different* binary image than my
scipy < 50 baseline — the rolling local mean is sensitive to the
floor-texture grading near the lower row of markers, fragmenting the
black ring or shifting the bbox.  Need to dump `s_binary` from the
detector and overlay on the original PGM to confirm.

Action: next session, expose a binding `sentai.markers._diag_binary()`
that returns the binarized buffer for the most recent detect.  Save
PGM + overlay PNG.  Tune block size or threshold offset based on what's
seen.

### Open problem 2: PnP perspective-Z bias

Bench test on saved frames shows WhyCon single-marker Krajník PnP
(`z = fx * D / (2*axis_a) * ANNULUS_FACTOR`) gives accurate depth when
the marker is near image center (frame 1 marker: axis_a=24.20, z=0.756
matches drone EKF z=0.78), but underestimates 10-30% when the marker
is offset to the image edge (axis_a inflated by perspective ellipse
stretching).  The 28% bias I measured earlier was on an off-center
marker, not a systematic constant — depends on marker pixel position.

**Fix path**: replace single-marker depth-from-ring with a coplanar
multi-marker PnP that uses pixel positions of all 6 markers + their
known world coordinates.  Either DLT-on-homography or ePnP on the
shared plane.  ~100-200 LoC C++; filed as W21-T4c future-work.

### Open problem 3: VPE feedback loop crashes drone (iter-13)

When VPE forwarder was enabled (ExtPos canal 0 or ExtPose canal 1), cf2
EKF received PnP-derived (biased-low) Z → "I'm too low, climb" → motors
climb → markers shrink in image → axis_a smaller → PnP under-estimates
more → cf2 climbs more → markers leave FOV → no PnP correction → motor
watchdog crash.  Positive feedback from biased PnP.

Iter-13: drone climbed past commanded Z_HOLD=0.78 to z=1.18 m, then
fell to ground.  GT confirms.

ExtPose canal 1 (with identity quaternion) ALSO yawed drone aggressively
(from ~46° actual to commanded 0° → motor command violence).

Both formats disabled in the current orchestrator; VPE re-enable
GATED on fixing PnP-Z bias (problem 2).  cf2 SITL has a sim baro
(`air_pressure_sensor` in model.sdf.jinja), drift ~0.1 m/14 s during
SAMPLE — bounded enough for sample-collection without VPE.

### Open problem 4: cf2 SITL altitude hold drifts upward without VPE

Even with VPE disabled (iter-17, iter-19), cf2 SITL drone CONTINUOUSLY
CLIMBS during the 14 s SAMPLE phase, even while `hover(0, 0, 0,
z_absolute=0.78)` is sent at 30 Hz.  GT data iter-19: drone went from
z=0.48 (post-takeoff) → 0.61 → 0.77 → 1.14 m over 20 s.  cf2 EKF z
tracked actual (both said z≈0.78 at one snapshot, but actual peaked at
1.14).

Could be cf2 firmware's altitude PID quirk, or the Generic Setpoint
hover() z_absolute isn't enforced strictly by the cf2 controller, or
baro miscalibration in SIM.  Investigation needed.

Operator note 2026-05-21: real drone has NO BAROMETER.  Vision-derived
Z is the only altitude reference.  Without VPE working, cf2 has no z
input on real HW.  Means VPE is mandatory for production bringup —
problem 2 (PnP-Z accuracy) MUST be solved before this calibration is
deployable.
